## Production rules

- **Long videos:** 3 acts — question, measurement, consequence. Show the real receipt/SHA; never claim verification beyond the stage completed.
- **Shorts:** one number, one visual, one sentence of context; end with “full breakdown on the channel.”
- **Thumbnails:** 3–5 words, high contrast; keep the violet/cyan identity.
- **Captions:** burn or upload the SRT generated by edge-tts/ElevenLabs.
- **Spanish variants:** reuse visuals; translate title/description/SRT; publish as a second audio track or separate videos.
- **Community:** post each long to Lean Zulip, Mathlib, r/lean, Hacker News only when relevant; no drive-by spam.
- **Week 6 caveat:** if certification has not passed by then, retitle to “Why Certification Is Hard” and show the stage ladder instead of claiming success.

## Monetization checkpoints

- Weeks 1–4: build library, playlists, channel trailer; no monetization pressure.
- Weeks 5–8: open GitHub Sponsors/Patreon; publish a “build audit” report.
- Weeks 9–12: pitch 5 sponsors with analytics; test a members-only build log.
- Track: CTR 5–8%, AVD 40%+, returning viewers, subscribers per video.
